KINMEN AROUND-THE-ISLAND BIKE TOUR ・ 5D4N

金門單車環島 5 天 4 夜Kinmen by Bike · 5 Days 4 Nights

環大金門一圈，再騎上金門大橋到小金門。閩南古厝、洋樓、戰地坑道一路串起來，每天騎 10–40 公里，留足時間停下來看。A loop around Kinmen plus a ride over the Kinmen Bridge to Lesser Kinmen — Minnan villages, colonial mansions and Cold War tunnels, 10–40 km a day with plenty of time to stop.

★ 風景名點★ Scenic highlights

★慈湖Cihu LakeD2

金門最知名的夕陽點，堤岸上看落日、晚霞染紅湖面，冬季還有成群鸕鶿歸巢。Kinmen's classic sunset spot: the sky turns the lake red, and in winter flocks of cormorants fly home to roost.

最佳：傍晚 16:45–17:30Best: 4:45–5:30 pm
★金門大橋Kinmen BridgeD2

台灣第一座長距離跨海大橋，騎在海面上 4.7 公里，一邊大海一邊廈門天際線。Taiwan's first long sea-crossing bridge: 4.7 km above open water, with the Xiamen skyline on the horizon.

避開強風時段Avoid strong-wind hours
★建功嶼Jiangong IsletD1

退潮時海中浮現石板步道，走上小島看延平郡王雕像與潮間帶。At low tide a stone causeway rises from the sea, leading to the islet and its tidal flats.

退潮前後 1 小時±1 h around low tide
★水頭聚落・得月樓Shuitou Village & Deyue TowerD1

閩南紅磚古厝與南洋風洋樓並存，得月樓是金門最具代表性的槍樓。Red-brick Minnan houses side by side with colonial-style mansions; Deyue Tower is Kinmen's iconic gun tower.

最佳：午後光線Best: afternoon light
★莒光樓Juguang TowerD1

金門地標，三層宮殿式樓閣，夜間打燈倒映在水池中。The island's landmark: a three-storey palace-style tower reflected in its pond when lit at night.

最佳：入夜後Best: after dark
★翟山坑道Zhaishan TunnelD1

花崗岩中挖出的 A 字型水道，光影與水面倒影非常震撼。An A-shaped waterway carved out of granite; the light and reflections are stunning.

全天皆宜Any time
★北山古洋樓・播音牆Beishan Mansion & Broadcast WallD3

古寧頭戰役留下滿牆彈孔的洋樓，旁邊是對岸心戰廣播用的巨型播音牆。A mansion riddled with bullet holes from the 1949 battle, next to the giant wall once used for propaganda broadcasts.

上午較少人Quieter in the morning
★瓊林聚落Qionglin VillageD3

保存完整的蔡氏宗祠群與地下坑道，燕尾屋脊層層疊疊。Well-preserved Cai clan ancestral halls with layered swallowtail rooflines and a hidden tunnel.

全天皆宜Any time
★山后民俗文化村Shanhou VillageD3

十八棟二落大厝整齊排列，是金門最經典的閩南聚落景觀。Eighteen two-courtyard houses in neat rows — the quintessential Minnan village scene.

清晨、黃昏最美Best at dawn or dusk
★馬山觀測所Mashan Observation PostD4

大金門最北端的觀測站，望遠鏡裡對岸近在咫尺。The northernmost point of Kinmen — through the telescopes the mainland feels close enough to touch.

晴天能見度佳Clear days are best
★陳景蘭洋樓・成功海灘Chen Jing-lan Mansion & Chenggong BeachD4

金門最大洋樓，正面對著廣闊的成功沙灘。Kinmen's largest Western-style mansion, facing the wide sands of Chenggong Beach.

下午順光Best in afternoon
★太武山Mt. TaiwuD5

金門最高點，「毋忘在莒」勒石與俯瞰全島的視野。Kinmen's highest point, with the famous inscription and panoramic island views.

清晨涼爽Cool in early morning

每日行程Day by day

Day 1

抵達金門・金城水頭古厝散策Arrival · Jincheng & Shuitou heritage

15km

金門機場 → 翟山坑道 → 古崗湖 → 水頭聚落・得月樓 → 建功嶼 → 莒光樓 → 金城模範街Airport → Zhaishan Tunnel → Gugang Lake → Shuitou Village & Deyue Tower → Jiangong Islet → Juguang Tower → Jincheng Old Town

  1. 上午AM
    機場取車Pick up bike at airport

    租車行多可送車到機場，或在金城車站租 K-Bike。Many shops deliver to the airport, or rent a K-Bike at Jincheng Station.

  2. 上午AM
    翟山坑道Zhaishan Tunnel

    花崗岩鑿出的 A 字型戰備水道，涼爽好拍。An A-shaped wartime boat tunnel blasted out of granite — cool and photogenic.

  3. 中午Noon
    古崗湖・水頭聚落Gugang Lake · Shuitou

    水頭吃午餐，逛得月樓與僑鄉洋樓群。Lunch in Shuitou, then Deyue Tower and the overseas-Chinese mansions.

  4. 下午PM
    建功嶼Jiangong Islet

    退潮時可走過石板路上島，出發前查潮汐。Walk across the stone causeway at low tide — check the tide table.

  5. 傍晚Eve
    莒光樓・模範街Juguang Tower · Old Street

    金門地標夜景，後浦老街吃晚餐。Kinmen's landmark lit up at night; dinner in the Houpu old quarter.

H
金城鎮（後浦老街周邊）Jincheng Town (Houpu old quarter)

餐廳、租車行與 K-Bike 金城車站都在這，連住兩晚不用搬行李；想住古厝可改水頭聚落民宿。Restaurants, bike shops and the K-Bike Jincheng Station are all here. Stay two nights so you don't move luggage; for a heritage house, pick a B&B in Shuitou Village.

Day 2

騎上金門大橋・烈嶼環島Kinmen Bridge & Lesser Kinmen loop

40km

金城 → 金門大橋 → 湖井頭戰史館 → 將軍堡 → 勇士堡・鐵漢堡 → 陵水湖 → 九宮坑道 → 八達樓子 → 金門大橋 → 慈湖 → 金城Jincheng → Kinmen Bridge → Hujingtou Museum → General's Fort → Warrior & Iron Man Forts → Lingshui Lake → Jiugong Tunnel → Badalouzi → Kinmen Bridge → Cihu Lake → Jincheng

  1. 上午AM
    金門大橋Kinmen Bridge

    全長約 5.4 公里，設有人行道與自行車道，橋上看海景。About 5.4 km long with a walkway and bike lane — sea views all the way.

  2. 上午AM
    湖井頭戰史館Hujingtou Museum

    烈嶼西北角，望遠鏡直看廈門。On the NW tip of Lesser Kinmen; telescopes look straight at Xiamen.

  3. 中午Noon
    將軍堡・勇士堡General's & Warrior Forts

    西岸據點與坑道，午餐吃芋頭料理。West-coast strongpoints and tunnels; try taro dishes for lunch.

  4. 下午PM
    陵水湖・九宮坑道Lingshui Lake · Jiugong Tunnel

    湖畔生態、戰備地下碼頭。Lakeside wildlife, then an underground wartime harbor.

  5. 傍晚Eve
    慈湖Cihu Lake

    過橋回大金門，慈湖堤岸看夕陽與鸕鶿。Back over the bridge for sunset and cormorants on the Cihu dyke.

H
金城鎮（同前一晚）Jincheng Town (same as night 1)

連住第二晚，回程晚一點也輕鬆。Second night in the same place, so a later return is no stress.

Day 3

古寧頭戰地・瓊林到沙美Guningtou battlefield to Shamei

30km

金城 → 北山古洋樓・播音牆 → 古寧頭戰史館 → 雙鯉湖 → 瓊林聚落 → 陽翟老街 → 沙美老街 → 山后Jincheng → Beishan Mansion & Broadcast Wall → Guningtou Battle Museum → Shuangli Lake → Qionglin Village → Yangzhai Old Street → Shamei Old Street → Shanhou

  1. 上午AM
    北山・古寧頭Beishan · Guningtou

    彈痕累累的北山洋樓、播音牆，古寧頭戰史館。Bullet-scarred Beishan mansion, the propaganda broadcast wall and the battle museum.

  2. 上午AM
    雙鯉湖Shuangli Lake

    濕地小歇，賞鳥。A wetland break for birdwatching.

  3. 中午Noon
    瓊林聚落Qionglin Village

    金門最完整的宗祠群之一，地下坑道可參觀。One of Kinmen's finest clusters of ancestral halls, with a visitable tunnel.

  4. 下午PM
    陽翟・沙美老街Yangzhai · Shamei streets

    懷舊老街、電影場景，喝杯咖啡。Retro streets and old film sets — stop for coffee.

  5. 傍晚Eve
    山后Shanhou

    入住古厝，晚上看滿天星。Check into a heritage house and enjoy the starry sky.

H
金沙鎮（山后、沙美一帶）Jinsha Town (Shanhou / Shamei)

山后民俗文化村內的古厝民宿最有氣氛；沙美老街周邊選擇也多。Heritage-house B&Bs inside Shanhou Folk Village have the most atmosphere; Shamei has more choices.

Day 4

東北海岸前線・山外成功Northeast frontline to Shanwai

25km

山后 → 獅山砲陣地 → 馬山觀測所 → 沙美 → 山外 → 榕園 → 陳景蘭洋樓 → 成功海灘 → 山外Shanhou → Shishan Howitzer Base → Mashan Observation Post → Shamei → Shanwai → Banyan Garden → Chen Jing-lan Mansion → Chenggong Beach → Shanwai

  1. 上午AM
    獅山砲陣地Shishan Howitzer Base

    坑道內的 8 吋榴砲，有定時砲操表演（出發前查時刻）。8-inch howitzers inside a tunnel, with scheduled gun drills (check times).

  2. 上午AM
    馬山觀測所Mashan Observation Post

    距離對岸最近的觀測點之一。One of the closest observation points to mainland China.

  3. 中午Noon
    沙美・山外Shamei · Shanwai

    沿縣道南下，山外吃廣東粥或燒餅。Ride south and lunch on Cantonese congee or sesame buns in Shanwai.

  4. 下午PM
    榕園・陳景蘭洋樓Banyan Garden · Chen Jing-lan

    老榕樹群與金門最大洋樓。Ancient banyans and Kinmen's largest Western-style mansion.

  5. 傍晚Eve
    成功海灘Chenggong Beach

    看海後回山外。Sea views, then back to Shanwai.

H
金湖鎮（山外市區）Jinhu Town (Shanwai)

全島生活機能最好的鎮區之一，小吃多，隔天離太武山、機場都近。One of the island's liveliest towns with plenty of food; close to Mt. Taiwu and the airport for the last day.

Day 5

太武山晨登・賦歸Mt. Taiwu hike & departure

10km

山外 → 太武山（登山口停車，步行上山）→ 金門機場還車Shanwai → Mt. Taiwu (park at trailhead, hike up) → return bike at airport

  1. 清晨Early
    太武山Mt. Taiwu

    步道來回約 2–2.5 小時，看「毋忘在莒」勒石與全島景色。About 2–2.5 h round trip to the famous 'Wu Wang Zai Ju' inscription and island views.

  2. 中午Noon
    午餐・伴手禮Lunch & souvenirs

    山外或沿途採買。Shop in Shanwai or along the way.

  3. 下午PM
    金門機場Kinmen Airport

    還車，搭機返台。Return the bike and fly home.

H
—（賦歸）— (fly home)

騎乘短，上午留給太武山與買伴手禮（貢糖、高粱、一條根）。A short ride: mornings are for Mt. Taiwu and souvenirs (peanut candy, kaoliang liquor, Yitiaogen balm).

騎乘須知Riding tips

🚲 租車🚲 Bike rental

金城、機場周邊有多家租車行。也可用 K-Bike 公共自行車（悠遊卡、一卡通可用），但長途建議租變速車或電輔車。

Several shops around Jincheng and the airport. K-Bike public bikes take EasyCard/iPASS, but for long days rent a geared bike or e-bike.

🌉 金門大橋🌉 Kinmen Bridge

全長約 5.4 公里，設有人行道與自行車道；橋面最高約 47 公尺，有段長緩坡，慢慢騎。

About 5.4 km with a walkway and bike lane; the deck rises to ~47 m, so expect a long gentle climb.

⚠️ 只走既有道路⚠️ Stay on the road

部分區域仍有未清除地雷，不要進入標示禁止進入的區域。

Some areas still have uncleared mines — never enter signed no-entry zones.

🌇 天黑得早🌇 Early sunset

11 月約 17:20 日落，前後燈一定要帶。

Sunset is around 5:20 pm in November — always carry lights.
