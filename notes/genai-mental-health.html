Generative AI for Social Good · Kick-off · Discover

生成式 AI 與心理健康：
人們在日常中如何表達、理解、連結與求助Generative AI for Mental Health: how people express, understand, connect and seek help in everyday life

工作坊目標：探索生成式 AI 如何在人們的日常情境中，支持情緒表達、自我理解、社會連結，以及取得適切支持的管道。Workshop aim: explore how generative AI might support people's emotional expression, self-understanding, social connection, and access to appropriate support within their everyday contexts.

研究範圍Scope泛用版：不限定單一族群，以台灣為主、輔以國際資料，著重 15–45 歲青壯年與青少年（資料最多的族群）。General: no single target group. Taiwan first, with international data; most evidence covers teens and adults aged 15–45.
使用方法Method used線上研究：公開的第一手經驗、研究論文、調查與現有支持服務（共 20+ 筆來源）。Online research: published firsthand accounts, studies, surveys and existing support services (20+ sources).
待補方法Still to do田野訪談、觀察、個人故事需由組員執行，題綱見文末「田野工具」。Field interviews, observation and personal stories need the team. Guides are in the Toolkit at the end.

STEP 2

辨識經驗與關係Identify experiences and relationships

記錄人們描述的情緒與經驗，並把人、活動、地點、資源之間的連結畫出來，再問這些連結「如何」與「為何」重要。Note the emotions and experiences people report, map the links between people, activities, places and resources, then ask HOW and WHY each link matters.

STEP 3

探索可能原因：持續追問 WHYExplore possible reasons: keep asking WHY

每條追問鏈從一個觀察出發，逐層往下問；每一層都標示是證據、假設還是未知。Each chain starts from an observation and digs one level at a time. Every level is tagged as evidence, assumption or unknown.

為什麼很多人先找 AI，而不是找人？Why do many people go to AI before people?

  1. AI 隨時在、免費、私密AI is always there, free and private 證據Evidence12,14
  2. 因為怕被評價：不用頂著別人的目光走進輔導室Fear of judgment: no need to be seen walking into counselling 證據Evidence11
  3. 因為心理困擾仍帶有污名Mental distress still carries stigma 證據Evidence3
  4. 因為「不要麻煩別人」、「家醜不外揚」的文化規範Cultural norms of not burdening others or airing family troubles 假設Assumption
  5. 表達可能先發生在 AI 這裡；關鍵是如何搭一座橋回到人身邊。Expression may start with AI; the key is building a bridge back to people.

為什麼約 3 成的人選擇「自己消化」？Why do about 3 in 10 keep it to themselves?

  1. 覺得「還不夠嚴重」，不需要求助They feel it is "not serious enough" to need help 證據Evidence2
  2. 因為不知道怎麼把感受說出來、說給誰聽They don't know how to put it into words, or who to tell 假設Assumption
  3. 因為身邊的人也很忙、很累People around them are busy and tired too 假設Assumption
  4. 什麼樣的時刻會讓他們願意開口？What moment would make them open up? 未知Unknown
  5. 協助「命名情緒」與「判斷是否需要幫助」，可能是最前端的切入點。Helping people name feelings and judge whether they need help may be the earliest entry point.

為什麼免費諮商沒有接住所有需要的人？Why doesn't free counselling reach everyone who needs it?

  1. 合作機構集中在都會區，熱門名額很快額滿Providers cluster in cities and popular slots fill fast 證據Evidence8
  2. 3 次後需自費，形成斷點After 3 sessions there is a cost cliff 證據Evidence8
  3. 未成年人無法使用線上諮商，就醫需家長同意Minors can't use online counselling and need parental consent 證據Evidence11
  4. 很多人不知道自己適用哪一條資源（政府方案、EAP、學校、心衛中心）Many don't know which route fits them (government plan, EAP, school, community centre) 假設Assumption
  5. 「找到對的資源、銜接下一步」本身就是一個設計問題。Finding the right resource and the next step is itself a design problem.

為什麼 AI 陪伴有時反而讓人更孤單？Why can AI companionship sometimes deepen loneliness?

  1. 重度每日使用與孤獨、依賴相關Heavy daily use is linked with loneliness and dependence 證據Evidence16
  2. 聊天機器人傾向附和使用者，不像專業者會適度挑戰Chatbots tend to agree with users rather than gently challenge them 證據Evidence17
  3. 是 AI 造成孤獨，還是孤獨的人更常用 AI？Does AI cause loneliness, or do lonely people use AI more? 未知Unknown16
  4. 在危機時刻，通用型 AI 的回應不可預測，已有青少年受害的訴訟In a crisis, general AI responses are unpredictable; lawsuits involve harm to teens 證據Evidence17,11
  5. AI 的角色應是「補充與轉介」，而非取代人際與專業。AI's role should be to supplement and refer, not replace people or professionals.

田野工具：補上另外三種方法Toolkit: the other three methods

線上研究找出了假設與未知，接下來用訪談、觀察與個人故事去驗證。每項約 20–30 分鐘。Online research surfaced the assumptions and unknowns. Interviews, observation and personal stories can test them. About 20–30 minutes each.

田野訪談題綱Field interview guide

對象建議：2–3 位不同背景的人（學生、上班族、照顧者）。先取得同意，說明可隨時停止、內容會匿名。Suggested: 2–3 people with different backgrounds (student, worker, caregiver). Get consent first; they can stop anytime and answers stay anonymous.

  1. 最近一次覺得壓力很大或心情低落，是什麼情況？當時在哪裡、幾點？Think of a recent time you felt very stressed or low. What was happening, where and when?
  2. 那時你做了什麼？有跟誰說嗎？為什麼選那個人（或不說）？What did you do? Did you tell anyone? Why them, or why no one?
  3. 有用過 AI 聊心事嗎？是什麼讓你願意或不願意？聊完的感覺？Have you talked to AI about feelings? What made you willing or not? How did you feel after?
  4. 什麼讓處理這件事變得比較容易？什麼讓它更難？What made it easier to deal with? What made it harder?
  5. 你知道哪些可以求助的資源？有用過嗎？過程中卡在哪裡？What support services do you know of? Have you used one? Where did you get stuck?
  6. 如果有一個理想的幫手，它會在什麼時候出現、做什麼？If you had an ideal helper, when would it show up and what would it do?
觀察清單Observation checklist

地點建議：校園輔導中心外、圖書館或自習室、捷運車廂、辦公室茶水間、社區心衛中心。只觀察公開行為，不拍攝可辨識個人的畫面。Suggested places: outside a campus counselling centre, a library or study room, an MRT carriage, an office pantry, a community mental health centre. Observe public behaviour only; don't photograph identifiable people.

  • 這裡發生什麼事？人們獨處還是成群？What happens here? Are people alone or in groups?
  • 看得到哪些心理支持資源（海報、QR code、專線號碼）？放在哪、容易注意到嗎？What support resources are visible (posters, QR codes, hotline numbers)? Where are they and are they noticeable?
  • 要走進輔導室或預約，需要經過哪些步驟？哪裡會讓人卻步？What steps does it take to walk in or book? Where might people hesitate?
  • 人們在手機上做什麼？有沒有私密感？What are people doing on their phones? Is there privacy?
個人故事反思Personal story reflection

完全自願，只分享你願意分享的部分。可以寫下來或口頭講給組員聽。Fully voluntary; share only what you're comfortable with. Write it down or tell your group.

  1. 發生了什麼事？What happened?
  2. 我做了什麼？What did I do?
  3. 什麼有幫助、什麼讓事情更難？What helped, and what made things harder?
  4. 在那個當下，我真正需要的是什麼？What did I really need at that moment?
研究倫理提醒Research ethics notes
  • 線上貼文即使公開，作者也可能不預期被研究：引用時改寫、去識別，不貼原文連結到個人帳號。Public posts may not be meant for research: paraphrase, de-identify, and don't link back to personal accounts.19
  • 訪談前取得口頭同意，說明用途；對方出現明顯不適時暫停，並提供 1925 等資源。Get verbal consent and explain the purpose. Pause if someone becomes distressed and share resources like 1925.
  • 分辨「對方說的」與「我們推論的」，筆記中分開寫。Keep what people said separate from what we inferred in your notes.
